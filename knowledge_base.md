- We buy cars directly even if the customer does not buy a car from us.

## 5. DEALERSHIP POLICIES & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

#### Q: Do you offer home delivery?
**A:** Yes! We offer free home delivery up to 50 miles from Bellevue. Beyond 50 miles, a transport fee of $2.50 per mile applies. The customer must sign all finance/purchase documents digitally prior to delivery.

#### Q: What is your return or exchange policy?
**A:** We offer a **3-day or 150-mile money-back guarantee** on all pre-owned vehicles. If you don't love it, bring it back within 3 days or 150 miles (whichever comes first) for a full refund or exchange. New vehicles are subject to manufacturer terms and cannot be returned once registered, but may qualify for state lemon laws.

#### Q: Can I buy out my lease early?
**A:** Yes, you can request an early buyout quote at any time. Contact our finance department directly at (425) 555-0190 to get your current buyout figure including taxes and fees.

#### Q: Can I get financing with bad credit or past bankruptcy?
**A:** Yes! We work with over 15 lenders specializing in subprime financing. We can help secure approvals for customers with past bankruptcies, repossessions, or low credit scores. A co-signer or larger down payment may be required.

#### Q: Do you charge dealer markups over MSRP?
**A:** No. We sell all new vehicles at MSRP or lower. We do not add "market adjustments" or mandatory dealer packages.

#### Q: Can I hold a vehicle?
**A:** Yes, we can hold any in-stock vehicle for up to 48 hours with a refundable **$500 holding deposit**. Contact your sales advisor to place a hold.

#### Q: What do I need to bring to buy a car?
**A:** Please bring:
1. A valid Driver's License.
2. Current Auto Insurance card (must list policy details).
3. Proof of income (most recent paystub or bank statements if self-employed).
4. Proof of residence (recent utility bill).
5. Preferred payment method (cashier's check, credit card up to $5,000, or banking details).
